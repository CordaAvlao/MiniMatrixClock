Mini Matrix Clock (V6 - Smart Monitor & Startup Fix)
----------------------------------

INSTRUCTIONS:

1. Double-click "MiniClock.exe".
2. Startup Fix: 
   - Enabling "Run on Startup" now uses a NEW registry key ("MiniClock_Auto").
   - This should force Windows to enable it, even if you previously disabled it.
   - Check Task Manager > Startup to confirm "MiniClock_Auto" is present and enabled.

3. Background Fix:
   - The clock now continuously monitors the background color.
   - It will FORCE opacity (black) if the background is dark, to prevent transparency issues.
